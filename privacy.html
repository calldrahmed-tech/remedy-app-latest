Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Smart Remedy AI ("the app", "we") is a clinical reference and case-support tool for homeopathic doctors. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — name and email address, collected when a doctor creates an account (by email/password or by signing in with Google).
  • Patient records (optional) — if a doctor chooses to use the "Save Visit" feature, we store the patient's name, age, contact number, symptoms, and the remedy given. This is entirely optional — nothing is saved unless the doctor taps "Save Visit."
  • Case text sent for AI analysis — when a doctor uses the "Analyse Case with AI" feature, the case description they typed is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a second opinion.
  • We do not currently use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising trackers of any kind.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To let a doctor log in and keep their own patient records private to their account.
  • To enforce the monthly limit on AI-assisted searches.
  • To generate an AI second opinion when a doctor requests one.

We do not use doctor or patient information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Google Firebase — handles login and stores account/patient data securely.
  • Anthropic — processes case text only when a doctor actively requests an AI second opinion.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Data Security

Account and patient data is stored in Google Firebase with access rules that restrict each doctor's patient records to that doctor's own account only — no other doctor, and no patient, can see another doctor's saved records. Data is encrypted in transit. As with any online service, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed 100% secure, and we continue to improve these protections over time.

5. Data Retention

Account and patient records are kept for as long as the account exists. A doctor can request deletion of their account and all associated patient records at any time by contacting us (see below).

6. Your Rights

  • You can request a copy of the data linked to your account.
  • You can request correction of inaccurate information.
  • You can request deletion of your account and patient records.

7. Payments

The app does not currently process card payments automatically. Any paid plan is handled manually (bank/wallet transfer verified by our team) — we do not store card numbers or banking credentials.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by licensed/practicing homeopathic doctors, not by children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the app's support contact provided within the app.
