Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

We want you to be happy with a paid plan. This policy explains when you can get your money back.

1. Refunds we will give

  • Within 7 days of payment, if you have used fewer than 10 AI Case Analyses on the paid plan.
  • At any time, if a fault on our side stopped you from using what you paid for and we could not fix it.
  • Double or mistaken charges, in full.

2. Refunds we do not give

  • After 7 days, when the plan has been used normally.
  • For a change of mind after you have used more than 10 AI Case Analyses.

3. If a payment fails

If your payment did not go through, you have not been charged and no plan is activated. If money left your account but Pro is not active after a few minutes, contact us with your payment receipt and we will fix it or refund you.

4. How to ask

Contact the support contact provided within the app, and include the email you log in with and your payment receipt or reference. We aim to reply within a few working days.

5. How we refund

Refunds go back to the method you paid with. The time it takes to appear depends on your bank or wallet. When a refund is given, the Pro plan ends on that day.
